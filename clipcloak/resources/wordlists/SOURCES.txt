common.txt.gz  – the 120,000 most frequent English and German words, lower case.
                 Derived from wordfreq 3.1.1 (Robyn Speer), https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
                 Data license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0

places.txt.gz  – place names made only of such words (cities with 15,000+ inhabitants worldwide and
                 1,000+ in Germany/Austria/Switzerland, countries, German states).
                 Derived from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/) via geonamescache 3.0.2,
                 license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
